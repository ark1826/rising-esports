1. Admin Portal Access & Session Management

The Admin Dashboard is the central command center for the entire platform. Access is restricted to verified administrators.

1 Access Route: Navigate to /admin/login via browser.
2 Authentication: Enter the master admin credentials provided by the platform lead.
3 Session Token: Sessions are secured using a JWT bearer token. If an action returns an unauthorized error, sign out and re-authenticate.
Security Best Practice: Never share admin credentials. When managing tournaments from public or LAN devices, always sign out from the top-right menu after concluding your session.

2. Tournament & Slot Scheduling Architecture

Rising Esports features four core tournament tiers, each linked directly to dynamic frontend categories:

Category Code Display Name Schedule Frequency Default Entry Fee Standard Prize Pool
SCRIMS Daily Scrims Multiple Slots Daily (2 PM, 4 PM, 6 PM, 8 PM) ₹60 / team ₹5,000 Total
WEEKLY WAR Weekly War Every Wednesday & Saturday ₹100 / team ₹10,000 Total
WEEKEND WAR Weekend War Saturdays & Sundays ₹150 / team ₹15,000 Total
GRANDS Grands Finals Monthly Invitational Finals Invite / Free ₹50,000+ Total

Step-by-Step: Creating a New Tournament Slot

  1. Click on the "Today Slots" tab or "Manage Slots" in Admin Dashboard.
  2. Click the purple "+ Add New Slot" button at top right to open the master modal.
  3. Fill out the mandatory schedule parameters:
    • Tournament / Match Title: e.g., RISING 1-3 GRIND SCRIMS
    • Date: e.g., 10 Oct 2026 (or use calendar picker)
    • Timing (IST): e.g., 02:00 PM
    • Category: Select from SCRIMS, WEEKLY WAR, WEEKEND WAR, or GRANDS
    • Lobby Name: e.g., LOBBY 1, LOBBY 2
    • Entry Fee: Enter price per team (e.g. 60)
    • Total Prize Pool: Enter numeric total (e.g. 5000)
    • Max Teams: Default is 20 teams per BGMI custom match
    • Map Rotation: Enter comma-separated maps, e.g., ERANGEL, RONDO, MIRAMAR
  4. Upload a tournament banner/hero image (JPEG or PNG, recommended 1200x600px).
  5. Set Visibility to Published (live immediately) or Draft (hidden for testing).
  6. Click "Save Changes". The new slot is instantly persisted to MongoDB and visible to all active clients.

3. Multi-Slot Rapid Scheduling (Duplicate Feature)

Organizing multiple scrim lobbies per day typically requires creating identical matches across different time slots (e.g., 2:00 PM, 3:30 PM, 5:00 PM, 7:00 PM). The Duplicate Slot engine eliminates manual re-entry.

1 Locate Source Slot: In the Manage Slots tab, find the template slot.
2 Click "Copy": Click the purple "Copy" button on the slot card.
3 Automated Duplication: The server clones the slot parameters (fee, prize pool, maps, distribution, rules, and image), clears old booked teams and room credentials, and marks the duplicate as Draft with (Copy) in the title.
4 Adjust Timing & Publish: Click "Edit Full" on the clone, update the timing (e.g., 04:00 PM) and lobby (e.g., LOBBY 2), change Visibility to Published, and save.
Operator Tip: A full day's roster of 6 custom scrim lobbies can be scheduled and published in under 90 seconds using this workflow!

4. Visibility Lifecycle: Draft vs. Published

To prevent incomplete schedules, preliminary testing, or pricing errors from reaching the player community, every tournament slot has a strict visibility state.

State Admin Panel Visibility Player Website Visibility Bookings Allowed
● Published Visible with green badge Live on Homepage, Category Lists, and Today Slots Yes (UPI & Wallet checkout active)
○ Draft Visible with yellow badge Hidden (Filtered out by backend API) No (Admin preview only)

One-Click Visibility Toggle:

  • On each slot card in the Admin Panel, an instant "Publish" / "Unpublish" button is available.
  • Clicking this button toggles the live status on the database via PATCH /api/slots/:id/publish without needing to open the full edit modal.

5. Registration Statuses & Slot Capacity Control

Admin can set custom registration states to reflect the real-time stage of the custom room:

  • Open: Registration is active and teams can register and pay.
  • Upcoming: Announced for future dates; countdown displayed.
  • Full: All 20 team slots booked; automatically triggers when bookedCount = maxTeams.
  • Ongoing / Live: Match is currently in progress; ID & Password locked.
  • Completed: Match concluded; ready for points table calculation.
  • Cancelled: Match called off; all registered teams queued for automatic refunds.

6. Team Management & Manual Slot Assignment

While players can register and pay directly through the client app, admins can manually assign teams (e.g., VIP teams, invited roster, or offline payments).

Assigning a Registered Team to a Slot:

  1. In the Today Slots panel, find the target slot card and click "+ Add Team" in the Registered Teams section.
  2. Select the team from the dropdown (displays Team Name, Team Tag, and Rising Registration ID).
  3. (Optional) Enter their initial drop location (e.g., Pochinki).
  4. Click "Assign Team to Slot". A confirmed paid booking is created and the fill rate is incremented.

Removing a Team from a Slot:

  • Click the red "Remove" button next to any team in the Registered Teams list.
  • The booking is deleted and their drop locations are cleared.

7. Map Rotations & Drop Locations Management

Custom lobbies feature 3-match rotations across standard competitive maps (Erangel, Miramar, Rondo, Sanhok, Vikendi). Ensuring teams have designated drop locations prevents drop clashes and disputes.

1 Access Drop Details Tab: In Admin Dashboard, click the "Drop Details" tab.
2 Select Active Slot: Choose the relevant tournament slot from the top dropdown.
3 Assign Match & Location: Select Match Number (Match 1, Match 2, or Match 3), Map (e.g., ERANGEL), Registered Team, and enter Drop Location (e.g., School, Pochinki, Rozhok, Georgopol).
4 Publish: Click "Save Drop Detail". The drop detail is immediately published to Screen 5 on the frontend.

8. Room ID & Password Security & Anti-Leak Protocol

Protecting custom room credentials is vital to prevent unregistered infiltrators from joining.

Parameter Admin Action Backend Protection
Room ID Entered in Slot Editor or Quick Creds bar Hidden from all public slot listings and unverified requests
Room Password Entered in Slot Editor or Quick Creds bar Hidden from all public slot listings and unverified requests
Time-Lock Window Configured via Match Timing Unlocked strictly 30 minutes before match time. Accessing earlier returns a 403 Too Early response.
Payment Gating Automatic User must have a confirmed paid booking record to reveal credentials.
Protocol Note: Always enter the Room ID and Password at least 35 minutes before match start. The system automatically handles time-based release to confirmed players at T-30 minutes.

9. Financial Operations: UPI Deposits & UTR Verification

When players deposit funds into their Rising Wallet or pay directly via UPI QR code, they submit their 12-digit bank UTR reference number.

Verification Workflow:

  1. Navigate to the "UPI Deposits" or "Bookings" tab.
  2. Filter by "Pending Verification" to view pending submissions.
  3. Copy the 12-digit UTR number using the "Copy" button.
  4. Cross-check the UTR and amount on your business UPI app (PhonePe, Paytm, Google Pay, or Bank Portal).
  5. If Payment Received: Click the green "Approve" button. The user's wallet is credited instantly or their tournament slot booking is confirmed.
  6. If Payment Not Received: Click the red "Reject" button. Enter an optional reason (e.g., "UTR not found in bank statement").
Fraud Prevention: Never approve a UTR without matching the exact transaction ID and amount in your receiving bank account statement. Duplicate UTR submissions are automatically flagged by the database.

10. Financial Operations: User Withdrawals & Wallet Adjustments

Players request payouts from their tournament winnings through the Wallet section.

Processing Payout Requests:

  1. Click on the "Wallet / Withdrawals" tab in Admin Dashboard.
  2. View the pending requests table showing: Player Team Name, Phone, Registered UPI ID, and Requested Amount.
  3. Open your business banking portal and initiate a UPI transfer to the player's UPI ID for the exact amount.
  4. Upon successful bank transfer, click "Approve Payout". The transaction is logged to the ledger as completed.
  5. Rejecting Invalid Withdrawals: If the UPI ID is invalid or fraudulent, click "Reject & Refund". The full withdrawal amount is automatically refunded back to the player's Rising Wallet balance.

Manual Wallet Balance Adjustment:

  • Under Wallet > Adjust Balance, select any player by Registration ID or Phone.
  • Select Credit (add funds) or Debit (deduct funds).
  • Enter Amount and Reason (e.g., "Tournament #1 First Place Prize Money").
  • Click "Apply Adjustment". Changes reflect immediately in the player's account.

11. Match Results Entry & Automatic Points Table Sync

After a tournament concludes, entering match statistics immediately syncs the leaderboard and Points Table on Screen 6.

1 Open Results Builder: Click "Enter Match Results" in the Results tab.
2 Enter Match Details: Input Match Name, Match Date, Timing, and Mode (Squad TPP).
3 Enter Placement & Finishes: Enter Team Names in order of finish (Position 1 to 16) and their finish/kill counts. The system automatically computes:
  • Placement Points: #1 = 10 pts, #2 = 6 pts, #3 = 5 pts, #4 = 4 pts, #5 = 3 pts, #6 = 2 pts, #7-8 = 1 pt.
  • Kill Points: 1 point per kill/finish.
  • Total Points: Placement Points + Kill Points.
4 Publish & Recalculate: Click "Save & Publish Results". The system saves the match record and recalculates cumulative team standings across the entire platform.

13. Emergency Escalation & Troubleshooting Guide

Issue / Incident Root Cause Action Procedure
Slot cannot be deleted Active paid bookings exist The server blocks accidental deletion to protect paid players. Refund or remove booked teams first, or pass ?force=true if authorized.
Players cannot see Room Credentials Time-lock active (T > 30 mins) or unpaid booking Confirm match start time is set correctly. Check if player payment is approved (paid status).
Disputed UTR / Double Claim Two players entered the same reference Search the UTR in All Transactions Ledger to identify the original claimant. Contact bank if unverified.
Match Cancelled due to BGMI Server Maintenance Server downtime Set slot status to Cancelled. Issue full wallet credits to all registered teams using the Adjustment tool.
